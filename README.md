## The entry

## Why this line

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **One whole funny line** — a complete joke, self-contained, that needs
   no setup or second line.
2. **A real turn** — a breakup letter from a fridge sounds like a gadget
   joke, then the punchline flips who is cold: the human, not the
   appliance.
3. **Tight** — 103 characters, every word earning its place, nothing
   left to trim.
4. **Fresh** — a smart appliance serving papers is not a recycled setup,
   and "the cold one" lands as a double meaning rather than a stock pun.
5. **Clear on first read** — the joke lands the moment the last word is
   read.
